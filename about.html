About Arsenii

Senior Technical Program Manager, Bay Area

Arsenii Samoilov is a Senior Technical Program Manager with 19+ years leading enterprise programs at Intuit, Atlassian, Adobe, Salesforce, Roku, and Apple. He partners with executive leadership on AI adoption, compliance governance, monetization, and cross-functional delivery at scale.

His work spans scaling AI features to 60%+ adoption, building regulatory issue lifecycles with 96%+ on-time closure, and delivering $50M+ in revenue through growth and bundling programs. He holds Product School, Certified Scrum Master, and Software Product Management credentials, with PMP certification in progress.

Beyond work

Arsenii volunteers teaching kids in San Jose schools how to cook and coaches youth hockey. He trained at Le Cordon Bleu and still runs a culinary site at chefarsenii.com. His photography portfolio lives on this site.
